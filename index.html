PURPOSE

Built for the moments touch isn't an option

MotionOS started as one engineer's attempt to apply computer vision to a real problem — and grew into a system with a few very different use cases.

♿

Accessibility

For people with motor disabilities, paralysis, or conditions like ALS, MotionOS offers a way to operate a computer using only natural movement — no grip, no pressure, no precision required from a joystick or switch.

Independent access
✛

Sterile environments

In operation theatres, diagnostic labs and rehab centers, touching a keyboard or mouse isn't always allowed. MotionOS lets doctors and staff control a screen from a distance, hands free of contact and contamination.

Zero-touch clinical use
◈

AI & CV skill-building

Every part of MotionOS — landmark detection, gesture classification, gaze estimation — is a working application of computer vision, built to bridge classroom knowledge and a real, running system.

Applied learning
PIPELINE

From webcam frame to OS action

A single video stream is enough — everything else happens in software, frame by frame.

01
